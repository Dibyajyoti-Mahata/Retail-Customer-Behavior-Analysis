Retail Customer Behavior Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project presents an end-to-end retail customer behavior analysis aimed at uncovering purchasing patterns, customer segments, and product performance to support data-driven business decisions.

The objective was to transform raw retail transaction data into meaningful business insights that can help improve customer engagement, optimize marketing strategies, and increase revenue.



# 🎯 Business Problem

Retail businesses collect large volumes of customer transaction data, but converting this data into actionable business decisions remains a challenge.

This project addresses key business questions such as:

- Which customer segments generate the highest revenue?
- Which product categories perform best?
- Does the subscription program influence customer spending?
- Which products depend heavily on discounts?
- What opportunities exist to improve profitability and customer engagement?

# 📈 Business Insights

### Customer Insights

- Male customers generated significantly higher revenue.
- Young Adults contributed the highest overall revenue.
- Loyal customers represented the largest customer segment.

### Product Insights

- Clothing generated the highest revenue.
- Accessories were the second strongest category.
- Several products achieved consistently high customer ratings.

### Subscription Insights

- Subscribers and non-subscribers showed similar average purchase values.
- Current subscription benefits appear to have limited impact on customer spending.

### Pricing Insights

- Multiple products relied heavily on discounts.
- This indicates opportunities to improve pricing strategy and promotional planning.

---

# ⚠️ Business Problems Identified

- Revenue is heavily concentrated in one product category.
- Subscription program shows limited influence on purchasing behavior.
- Several products depend on discounts for sales.
- Lower-performing categories remain underutilized.
- Marketing efforts can be improved through customer segmentation.

---

# 💡 Strategic Recommendations

- Focus marketing campaigns on high-value customer segments.
- Strengthen cross-selling opportunities.
- Improve subscription benefits.
- Reduce dependency on excessive discounting.
- Promote highly rated products more aggressively.
- Use customer segmentation for personalized marketing.
